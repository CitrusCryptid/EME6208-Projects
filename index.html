About me

I’m focused on building technology that makes learning more engaging, intuitive, and genuinely effective. My work in education technology is driven by a belief that great learning experiences shouldn’t feel like systems to navigate... they should feel like environments people want to be in. Before transitioning into edtech, I worked in game development as a VFX artist, where I learned how visual feedback, interactivity, and immersion shape the way people engage with complex systems. That experience continues to inform my approach today: I design learning tools with the same intentionality as interactive experiences—where clarity, responsiveness, and motivation are core to the design. I’m particularly interested in how principles from games and creative technology can be applied to education to improve engagement, support skill development, and sustain long-term motivation. My goal is to build learning experiences that don’t just deliver information, but actively help people progress, experiment, and grow.
